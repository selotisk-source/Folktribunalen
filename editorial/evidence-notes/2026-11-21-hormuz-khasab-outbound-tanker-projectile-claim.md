## Reported event

Reuters reported that an outbound tanker transiting the Strait of Hormuz caught fire after being struck by an unknown projectile approximately eight nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman. The report attributes the initial incident information to the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency (UKMTO). No casualties or environmental damage were reported in the Reuters account.

## Separate party claim

Reuters also reported that Iran's Revolutionary Guards later offered a different explanation through Iranian state media: the tanker had hit a mine while attempting to transit the strait.

The mine explanation is a party claim. The Reuters report does not independently establish that a mine caused the fire. Keep it separate from UKMTO's reported description of an unknown projectile.
